DELIVERY & LOGISTICS

From warehouse to buyer

Delivery conditions can vary from one stock lot to another. Before confirming a transaction, the buyer and supplier should agree who organises transport, the delivery cost, timing and the responsibilities of each party.

How delivery works

Logistics should be agreed before the transaction is confirmed. The product offer provides the starting information for planning delivery.

1

Check the stock location

Review the warehouse location shown in the offer. This helps the buyer estimate transport distance, cost and possible delivery options.

2

Agree transport

The buyer and supplier confirm who will arrange transport and which delivery terms apply to the transaction.

3

Confirm before shipment

Delivery cost, destination, timing, documentation and responsibilities should be confirmed before the goods are released.

Who organises transport?

There is no single delivery model for every offer. Transport can be organised in different ways depending on the agreement between buyer and supplier.

OPTION 01

Buyer arranges transport

The buyer selects a carrier and organises collection from the supplier's warehouse according to the agreed collection conditions.

OPTION 02

Supplier arranges transport

The supplier organises shipment to the agreed destination and confirms the applicable transport cost and delivery conditions with the buyer.

OPTION 03

Separately agreed logistics

Buyer and supplier may agree another logistics solution appropriate to the stock lot, destination and commercial terms.

DELIVERY COST

Is transport included in the product price?

Not necessarily. Unless an offer explicitly states otherwise, the product price and delivery cost should be treated as separate commercial elements.

Transport cost can depend on the warehouse location, destination, number of pallets, total weight and volume, carrier, required delivery service and agreed delivery terms.

The final delivery cost should therefore be confirmed before the transaction is completed.

Delivery terms & Incoterms

Buyers and suppliers may use agreed Incoterms® rules to define transport responsibilities, costs and transfer of risk more clearly.

EXW

Ex Works

The buyer generally takes responsibility for transport from the agreed supplier location.

FCA

Free Carrier

The supplier delivers the goods to the carrier or agreed place under the conditions defined by the parties.

DAP

Delivered at Place

The supplier arranges transport to the agreed destination under the applicable agreed terms.

Other

Agreed terms

Other delivery terms may be used when appropriate for the transaction and agreed by both parties.

The applicable delivery rule, named place and version should be clearly agreed between the buyer and supplier. Sweety Pets does not select the Incoterm for the parties.

Responsibilities of the parties

The exact responsibilities depend on the commercial and delivery terms agreed for each transaction.

SUPPLIER

Before goods leave the warehouse

  • Confirm stock availability and agreed quantity.
  • Prepare the goods according to the agreed order.
  • Provide relevant commercial and shipment documentation.
  • Confirm collection or dispatch details.
  • Follow the agreed delivery terms.
BUYER

Before accepting delivery

  • Confirm the delivery destination and contact details.
  • Arrange transport when this is the buyer's responsibility.
  • Review the agreed transport and delivery conditions.
  • Be prepared to receive the shipment.
  • Check the delivered goods and report relevant issues promptly.

Delivery times

01

Deal confirmed

Quantity, price and delivery terms are agreed.

02

Goods prepared

Supplier prepares the stock for dispatch or collection.

03

Transport

Goods move according to the agreed logistics arrangement.

04

Delivery

Buyer receives and checks the shipment.

Important

Delivery times shown or discussed during a transaction may depend on stock preparation, carrier availability, route, destination and other logistics factors. Buyers and suppliers should confirm the expected dispatch and delivery schedule before completing the transaction.
