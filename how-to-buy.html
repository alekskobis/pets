How the buying process works

A closer look at each stage of a Sweety Pets B2B purchase.

1

Browse available stock

Visit the catalogue to discover clearance stock, overstock, seasonal collections, discontinued products, previous-design packaging and other available pet product inventory from European suppliers.

2

Send a request

Open the product page, review the available quantity, MOQ, price, warehouse location and delivery information, then submit a request for the quantity you are interested in purchasing.

3

Confirm commercial terms

Before the order is confirmed, the buyer and supplier agree the final quantity, price, VAT treatment, product availability, delivery method and other commercial conditions relevant to the transaction.

4

Arrange payment

After the order conditions have been agreed, the payment terms and required commercial documentation are confirmed before dispatch.

5

Organise delivery

Depending on the offer, transport may be arranged by the buyer, by the supplier or agreed separately. The goods are then prepared for collection or shipment from the stated warehouse location.

Before sending a request

Check the main commercial details of each stock lot.

Available quantity & MOQ

Each offer shows the available stock and minimum order quantity. Your requested quantity should meet the supplier's MOQ.

Price & VAT

Review the B2B offer price and VAT information. Final commercial conditions should be confirmed before the order is placed.

Warehouse & delivery

Check where the goods are located and whether transport is arranged by the buyer, supplier or according to separately agreed terms.
