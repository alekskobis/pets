ABOUT SWEETY PETS

A better way to move pet inventory across Europe

Sweety Pets is a European B2B pet inventory platform connecting suppliers with professional buyers looking for available stock, overstock, seasonal collections and discontinued pet products.

Why Sweety Pets exists

Pet businesses across Europe hold inventory that may no longer fit their current sales plans — even though the products can still have commercial value for another buyer.

Overstock, slow-moving products, previous collections, discontinued ranges and packaging changes can leave perfectly sellable products sitting in warehouses.

At the same time, retailers, distributors and other professional buyers are constantly looking for attractive products and new purchasing opportunities.

Sweety Pets is designed to bring these two sides together in one specialised B2B environment.

OUR IDEA

Available stock in one business can become a new opportunity for another.

Built for the pet industry

Instead of mixing thousands of unrelated product categories, Sweety Pets focuses specifically on B2B pet products and available inventory.

How the platform works

Sweety Pets connects supply and demand without turning inventory discovery into a complicated process.

SUPPLIERS

List available stock

Suppliers publish product lots with relevant B2B information including quantity, MOQ, price, warehouse location and available sales markets.

→
BUYERS

Discover and request

Professional buyers discover relevant inventory, review the offer and submit a request. Commercial and delivery conditions are then agreed as part of the B2B transaction.

Inventory with another opportunity

The platform is designed around products that remain commercially usable but require a new sales channel.

Overstock

Inventory exceeding current sales or distribution needs.

Slow-moving stock

Products that remain in warehouses longer than planned.

Seasonal collections

Previous or remaining seasonal product collections.

Discontinued products

Products or ranges that are no longer part of the supplier's main assortment.

Packaging changes

Sellable inventory remaining after packaging or branding updates.

Assortment changes

Products removed as companies optimise or change their product assortment.

Clearance stock

Inventory suppliers want to move through an additional B2B sales channel.

Available lots

Other business-ready pet product inventory offered as defined B2B lots.

OUR MISSION

Make existing inventory easier to discover

Our goal is to create a focused European B2B environment where pet industry suppliers can find additional demand for available stock and professional buyers can discover new inventory opportunities more efficiently.
