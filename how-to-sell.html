How the selling process works

A closer look at each stage of selling your inventory.

1

Create your supplier account

Register your business and provide the basic company information required to create a supplier profile. Registration gives you access to the supplier tools needed to manage your inventory and buyer requests.

2

Complete company verification

Before trading, company information may be reviewed to help maintain a professional B2B environment. Verification is intended to make it easier for buyers and suppliers to identify legitimate business partners.

3

Create an inventory lot

Add the product name, brand, category, condition, available quantity, minimum order quantity, B2B price, VAT information, warehouse location, available sales markets, photos and other relevant product details.

4

Receive buyer requests

When a buyer is interested in your stock, they can submit a request for the quantity they need. Requests can then be reviewed and managed through your supplier account.

5

Agree the commercial terms

Confirm the final quantity, price, VAT treatment, payment conditions, product availability, transport arrangements and any other conditions required to complete the B2B transaction.

6

Prepare and ship the goods

Once the transaction is confirmed, prepare the goods for collection or shipment. Delivery can be organised by the supplier, buyer or according to separately agreed logistics terms.

What can you list?

Sweety Pets is focused on helping European pet businesses find new buyers for available inventory.

Overstock & slow-moving stock

Products that remain in your warehouse longer than planned or inventory that exceeds your current distribution requirements.

Seasonal & discontinued products

Previous seasonal collections, discontinued ranges and products being replaced by newer models, designs or packaging.

Clearance inventory

Stock available for B2B clearance because of assortment changes, warehouse optimisation, packaging updates or other commercial reasons.
